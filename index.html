The Landscape

A young, fast-moving fintech scene with room to grow

Tunisia was the first country in Africa to pass a dedicated Startup Act, in April 2018 — a law that streamlined company creation, opened access to talent visas, and allocated public funding toward high-growth ventures. That early move helped seed a wave of fintech activity across payments, remittances and digital banking.

Yet financial inclusion still lags: fewer than 40% of Tunisians aged 15+ held a bank account as of the most recent national survey, and mobile money penetration remains in the single digits despite strong smartphone and internet adoption. That gap between infrastructure and inclusion is exactly the opportunity fintech is built to close.

The Central Bank of Tunisia (BCT) has responded with a regulatory sandbox launched in 2020, a dedicated fintech committee, and an internal technology hub (TCB-Lab) to test new payment rails and digital financial products before they reach the wider market.

Regulator

Banque Centrale de Tunisie

Runs the national fintech sandbox, chairs a dedicated fintech committee, and pilots new payment infrastructure through its TCB-Lab.

Policy

Startup Act (2018)

Africa's first startup law — simplified incorporation, talent mobility, and a public Fund of Funds supporting high-potential ventures.

Where the innovation happens

The pillars of Tunisian fintech

From payments infrastructure to inclusive banking, these are the areas where Tunisian fintech companies and institutions are pushing hardest.

₮

Digital Payments

Mobile wallets, QR payments and unified payment rails are expanding fast, led by both fintech startups and incumbents like La Poste Tunisienne, which already serves 6M+ customers.

🏦

Digital Banking

Traditional banks are modernizing core infrastructure — from cloud-based core banking platforms to digital-first account opening — to compete with agile new entrants.

🌍

Remittances

With a large Tunisian diaspora in Europe, cross-border remittance flows are a major use case for fintech innovation and a key driver of financial inclusion at home.

🛡️

RegTech & Compliance

As the BCT sandbox matures, tools for KYC, AML and regulatory reporting are becoming a growing niche for Tunisian fintech builders.

💳

Alternative Lending

Credit card penetration remains low (around 8%), opening space for alternative credit-scoring and lending models tailored to underserved segments.

🚀

Startup Support

A network of 53+ incubators, accelerators and support organizations — backed by public and international funding — helps founders go from idea to scale.

How we got here

Key milestones in the ecosystem

2018

The Startup Act

Tunisia becomes the first African country to pass a comprehensive Startup Act, simplifying company creation and unlocking public funding for high-growth ventures.

2020

Regulatory sandbox launched

The Central Bank of Tunisia opens a fintech regulatory sandbox and forms a dedicated fintech committee to safely test new financial products.

2020

Pandemic accelerates digital payments

Digital payment transaction volumes jump 34% during COVID-19 lockdowns, pulling forward years of behavioral change in a matter of months.

2022

Core banking modernization

Arab Tunisian Bank goes live on modern core banking technology, part of a broader wave of infrastructure upgrades across the sector.

2024–2025

Tunisia becomes MENA's 5th largest hub

With 1,450+ active startups, a 56% jump in active investors, and scaleup funding approaching $200M, Tunisia cements itself as a regional innovation power.

Incumbent

Talent
