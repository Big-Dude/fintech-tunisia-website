About this rate

This is the mid-market reference rate (the midpoint between buy and sell prices on international markets), from an open dataset updated daily. It is a reference, not a quote: banks, exchange offices and transfer services in Tunisia and abroad apply their own rates and fees.

The Central Bank of Tunisia publishes its own figure, the average of the previous day's interbank trades, which can differ slightly. The Tunisian dinar is not freely convertible and can't be bought outside Tunisia; read our guide to the euro–dinar rate for the rules.

Sending money to Tunisia? The rate you get depends on the provider. Compare transfer costs to see how many dinars actually arrive.
