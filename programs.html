Education & incubation for Tunisian fintech

Two tracks, one goal: building real fintech capability in Tunisia. Training built on operational examples, and hands-on support for early-stage ideas.

Education programsIncubation

Education programs

Courses built on real, operational fintech examples, for individuals and teams who want to move from theory to practice.

01

Digital payments fundamentals

How mobile wallets, QR payments and payment rails work, and how to build on top of them in Tunisia.

02

RegTech & compliance

KYC, AML and regulatory reporting essentials for teams working with the BCT framework.

03

Digital banking foundations

Core banking modernisation, digital onboarding and the move away from legacy infrastructure.

04

Fintech for founders

A practical crash course on building and funding a fintech startup in the Tunisian regulatory context.

Practical, cohort-based learning

Each program runs in small cohorts so participants get real feedback, not just lectures. Sessions mix short instruction with case studies drawn from Tunisian and regional fintech companies. Programs are open to individuals and company teams.

FormatCohort-based: small groups, real case studies, direct feedback.
Who it's forFrom first-time founders to compliance teams at established banks.
