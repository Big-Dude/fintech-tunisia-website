How incubation works

From idea to pilot

Our incubation track is built around Tunisia's real regulatory environment — not a generic startup playbook. That means working directly with the BCT sandbox process, connecting founders to banks and telcos willing to pilot new products, and pairing teams with mentors who've operated fintech products in Tunisia before.

We prioritize ideas addressing the country's biggest gaps: financial inclusion, digital payments infrastructure, and alternative credit for the underserved.
